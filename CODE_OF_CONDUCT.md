# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

SolSharp is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community. Be considerate,
assume good intent while discussing ideas, give actionable feedback, and focus criticism on code and behavior.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, sustained disruption, personal attacks, and publishing
someone else's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces. It also
applies when someone is publicly representing SolSharp or acting with project authority.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the **Contact owners** link on the
[SolSharp NuGet page](https://www.nuget.org/packages/SolSharp). Do not post sensitive details in a public issue.
Suspected software vulnerabilities follow the separate private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and impartially as possible, shared only as needed to investigate, and
handled with attention to the reporter's safety and privacy. Depending on severity and history, maintainers may
request a correction, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant
from project spaces. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/),
version 3.0, and its community enforcement guidance.
